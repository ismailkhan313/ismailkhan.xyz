## Hi, I'm Ismail Khan

I write about **agentic AI architecture** — how to actually design, build, and operate systems where LLM agents plan, use tools, and act with a meaningful degree of autonomy. Most of what's here is technical: architecture patterns, trade-offs, failure modes, and the engineering decisions that don't show up in the demo.

I also work with teams as a consultant, helping them go from "we have an AI proof of concept" to "we have a system we trust in production."
