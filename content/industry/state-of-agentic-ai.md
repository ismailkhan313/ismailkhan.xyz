> Starter post. Replace it with your own analysis. Industry posts can be shorter and more opinionated than architecture deep-dives; the goal is a clear point of view, not exhaustive coverage.

A good template for this section:

1. **What's being claimed**: the headline or announcement.
2. **What's actually true**: grounded in what you've seen implemented, not just marketed.
3. **What it means for teams building with this stuff**: the actionable takeaway.

Keep these tight. 400–800 words is usually enough to make one point well.
